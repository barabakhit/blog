Surah Al-Baqarah: Reflections on Guidance and Peace

By Baraa Bakhit on November 20, 2026

Surah Al-Baqarah is the longest chapter in the Quran and holds a special place in the hearts of believers. Reading and reflecting on its verses brings calm to the mind and clarity to daily life.

Key Themes in the Surah

The surah covers guidance, faith, patience, and practical rules that shape a balanced life. It also includes verse 255, known as Ayat al-Kursi, which many people recite for comfort and protection.
